# Trax

A shared board where the students of one school post the internships they find, track deadlines together, and get help choosing and applying.

## Language

**School**:
The one institution whose students use Trax.
_Avoid_: University, college, campus

**Classmate**:
A signed-in student of the School, shown by their full name from their school account, with one Course, a year and a section.
_Avoid_: User, member, student (when meaning a signed-in person), class (as a group)

**Moderator**:
A Classmate trusted to review Hidden Listings and close Listings on the board.
_Avoid_: Admin

**Course**:
A Philippine degree program, such as BSIT. Each Classmate has exactly one; each Listing names the Courses it accepts, and a Listing naming none is open to all Courses.
_Avoid_: Major, program, degree

**Internship**:
The real-world opportunity offered by a company.
_Avoid_: Job, posting, opportunity

**Listing**:
The School's saved record of one Internship, shown as a card on the board.
_Avoid_: Post, entry, card (a card is only how a Listing looks)

**Skill tag**:
A short keyword a Poster adds to a Listing to name a skill the Internship asks for.
_Avoid_: Keyword, requirement

**Poster**:
The Classmate who added a Listing, credited on it.
_Avoid_: Author, submitter, owner

**Deadline**:
The date and time, in Philippine time, after which applications to an Internship close.
_Avoid_: Due date, cutoff

**Rolling**:
A Listing with no Deadline because the company accepts applications until the Internship is filled. It is never Archived by date.
_Avoid_: Open-ended, no deadline

**Allowance**:
The pay an Internship offers, marked Paid (optionally with an amount), Unpaid or Not stated.
_Avoid_: Salary, stipend, pay

**OJT hours**:
The number of on-the-job training hours an Internship offers, shown on its Listing.
_Avoid_: Credit hours, duration (duration is the length in time)

### Listing states

**Open**:
The Listing is on the board and accepting applications.
_Avoid_: Active, live

**Hidden**:
The Listing was reported enough times to leave the board and awaits Moderator review.
_Avoid_: Flagged, suspended

**Archived**:
The Listing is closed for good, either because its Deadline passed or a Moderator closed it. It stays viewable by direct link.
_Avoid_: Closed, expired, deleted

### Progress

**Status**:
A Classmate's own progress on a Listing: Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offered or Rejected. Saved is always private; Applied and later are shown to other Classmates unless the Classmate hides them.
_Avoid_: Stage, state (state is for a Listing)

### Trust

**Verify**:
A Classmate's confirmation that they checked a Listing and it is accurate.
_Avoid_: Upvote, like, approve

**Report**:
A Classmate's claim that a Listing is closed, wrong or spam.
_Avoid_: Flag
